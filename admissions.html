How It Works

Admissions in five steps, most of it done over WhatsApp or email.

Applications are accepted year-round for the following January intake, though popular year groups do fill earlier. Start with an enquiry — a phone call, WhatsApp message, or the form on our contact page — and our admissions team will send the application pack and a shortlist of available assessment dates. Most families complete the full process, from first enquiry to enrollment confirmation, within three to four weeks.

Admission Process

Five steps, start to finish.

01

Make an Enquiry

Call, WhatsApp, or use the contact form. We'll send the application pack within one business day.

02

Submit Application

Complete the application form and provide the documents listed under Requirements below.

03

Assessment

An age-appropriate academic assessment and a short family interview, usually on the same visit.

04

Decision

Admission decisions are communicated within five business days of the assessment.

05

Enrollment

Confirm the place, complete enrollment paperwork, and receive your child's class placement and start date.

Requirements

What to bring to your application.

The list below reflects our typical requirements — final documentation can vary slightly by year group, and our admissions team will confirm exactly what's needed for your child when you make an enquiry.

  • Completed application form
  • Certified copy of the child's birth certificate
  • Two most recent academic reports (for Primary and above)
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Immunization/medical record
  • Transfer or recommendation letter from the previous school, where applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from families.

How do I apply?

Start with an enquiry via WhatsApp, phone, or our contact form — we'll send the application pack and available assessment dates within one business day.

What documents are required?

See the Requirements section above. Your exact document list will be confirmed by our admissions team based on your child's year group.

When do applications open?

Applications are accepted year-round for the following January intake. Popular year groups can fill before the priority assessment period, so earlier enquiries are recommended.

Can I visit the school?

Yes — campus visits are part of the assessment process, and standalone visits can be arranged separately. Contact admissions to schedule one.

How can I contact admissions?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. You can also call, email, or use the form on our Contact page.
