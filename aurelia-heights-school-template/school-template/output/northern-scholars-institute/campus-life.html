Campus Life

Life Beyond the Classroom

Sport, music, leadership and the everyday texture of a school day — what it actually feels like to be a student here.

Student Experience

The day doesn't end when the last class does.

Every student at Northern Scholars takes part in at least one club, sport, or ensemble outside the regular timetable — not as an extracurricular add-on, but as part of how the school expects students to spend their time. Sports fixtures, rehearsals, and society meetings happen most afternoons, and Founders' Day, our biggest event of the year, is planned almost entirely by the student council.
