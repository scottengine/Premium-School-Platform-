Who We Are

More Than a School. A Foundation for Life.

Aurelia Heights was founded on a simple premise: that rigorous academics and genuine character-building are not competing priorities. Our teachers plan around both. Classes stay small enough for a teacher to notice when a student is struggling before a report card does, and our pastoral structure means every child has an adult on campus who knows them well — not just their grades, but their week.

How We Teach

What actually makes the classroom different here.

Small-Group Learning

Core subjects capped at 22 students so every child is known, not just enrolled.

Science & Technology

Dedicated lab time from Primary 4 onward, not held back for Secondary.

Reading Culture

A whole-school reading hour, four mornings a week, no exceptions.

Leadership Development

Every senior student holds a real responsibility — house, club, or peer-mentoring role.

Creative Arts

Music, visual art and drama are timetabled subjects, not optional extras.

Digital Literacy

Applied computing and research skills embedded from Primary through A-Level.
